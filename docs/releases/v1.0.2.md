## AgentTally 1.0.2 (代理指示)

### New

- **Breathing dots.** Dots that wait on you — a permission request, a question, or a finished
  result you have not read — ease between hollow and solid every two seconds, each at its own
  pace. Working dots hold still. Turning on Reduce Motion in macOS keeps every dot still.

### Changed

- **The panel uses the system's regular Liquid Glass**, drawn together with its text, so macOS
  adapts both to whatever is behind the panel: text stays readable over dark and light desktops
  alike, and the glass keeps one look whether or not the panel has focus. The transparency slider
  now sets how much theme color tints the glass, and new installs start at 100% (bare glass).

### Fixed

- Choosing Dark (or Light) in Settings › Appearance › Mode now changes the glass too, instead of
  leaving light glass under dark-mode text.

## AgentTally 1.0.2（代理指示）

### 新增

- **呼吸指示灯**：等你处理的圆点（授权请求、提问、还没看的结果）每 2 秒在空心和实心之间缓慢过渡一次，
  各自节奏错开；工作中的圆点保持静止。打开系统的“减弱动态效果”后，所有圆点都保持静止。

### 变化

- **面板改用系统的常规液态玻璃**，并且和文字画在一起，系统会根据面板后面的内容同时调整玻璃和文字：
  深色、浅色桌面上文字都看得清，点击面板前后外观也不再变化。透明度滑杆改为控制玻璃上叠加多少主题色，
  新安装默认是 100%（纯玻璃）。

### 修复

- 在「设置 › 外观 › 模式」里选择深色（或浅色）后，玻璃也会跟着变，不会再出现浅色玻璃配深色模式文字的情况。
