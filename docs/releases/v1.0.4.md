## AgentTally 1.0.4 (代理指示)

### New

- **Floating notices.** While the floating indicator is on, each session that is working, needs
  you, or has an unread result gets a small glass notice under the capsule, in place of system
  notification banners. Click one to jump to its session; close it with ×.
  - A notice stays until its session goes idle, is read, or ends. A closed notice comes back when
    its session starts waiting for you, finishes, or takes up new work after finishing.
  - Newest activity first, up to the number set in Settings › General › Floating Notices (4 by
    default); "N more" under them opens the panel.
  - Drag the stack anywhere and it stays there; drop it back under the capsule to have it follow
    the capsule again. The notices step aside while the panel is open.

## AgentTally 1.0.4（代理指示）

### 新增

- **浮窗提醒**：悬浮指示灯开着时，工作中、等你处理或有未读结果的会话，会在胶囊下方各显示一个玻璃小浮窗，
  代替系统通知。点浮窗跳到对应会话，点 × 关闭。
  - 浮窗一直保留，直到会话闲置、已读或结束。关掉的浮窗会在会话开始等你、完成，或完成后又开始新任务时重新出现。
  - 最近有交互的排在前面，最多显示几条在「设置 › 通用 › 浮窗显示上限」里调（默认 4 条）；
    超出的显示为「还有 N 条」，点一下打开面板。
  - 整组浮窗可以拖到任意位置并记住；拖回胶囊下方会重新跟着胶囊走。面板打开时浮窗暂时隐藏。
