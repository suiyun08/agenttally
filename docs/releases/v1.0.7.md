## AgentTally 1.0.7 (代理指示)

### New

- **Feishu bridge conversations.** Claude Code and Codex sessions that the Feishu bridge
  (`lark-channel-bridge`) runs for a Feishu chat now stay listed between messages. The bridge starts
  a fresh process for every message, which used to end the session the moment it answered.
  - While a message runs, the session shows as working; when it finishes, its result stays in
    Active, with its notice, until you open it from AgentTally or send the bot your next message.
    Once seen and quiet for 10 minutes, it moves to Recent.
  - Clicking it opens its Feishu topic, or its group chat when the bot answers outside a topic.
    The topic link is not part of Feishu's documented links, so a Feishu update may turn it off.
  - A session leaves once its bot stops for more than 10 minutes or no longer holds the conversation.
- **Questions from Claude Desktop's wrap-up card.** When Claude Desktop ends a turn with a card that
  asks you something, the session shows "Needs Input" with that question, instead of "Done".

### Changed

- **A turn that leaves background work ends as Done.** It used to show "Background" in the working
  green, as if Claude were still busy. It now shows as Done, unread, with its notice, and a second
  line names the background tasks. When they wake Claude up, it shows Working again.
- **Nothing times out.** A question no longer turns idle after 60 minutes; it waits for your answer,
  overnight included.

### Fixed

- **Jumping to another app no longer reads Claude Desktop's conversation.** Claude Desktop marks the
  conversation its window shows as viewed when that window comes into view behind another app, for
  example when a click on a Codex notice brings Codex over your browser. AgentTally now counts that
  mark only when Claude Desktop was actually in front.

## AgentTally 1.0.7（代理指示）

### 新增

- **飞书桥接会话**：飞书桥接（`lark-channel-bridge`）替飞书聊天跑的 Claude Code 和 Codex 会话，两条消息之间也会一直显示。
  以前桥接每条消息都会单独启动一个进程，回答完进程一退出，AgentTally 就把会话当成结束了。
  - 处理消息时显示“工作中”；处理完，结果留在“活跃”里并弹出浮窗，直到你在 AgentTally 里点开它，或在飞书里给机器人发下一条消息。
    看过之后安静 10 分钟，移到“最近”。
  - 点开会直接打开对应的飞书话题；机器人不在话题里回复时，打开对应的群。
    打开话题用的是飞书没有公开文档的链接，飞书以后升级可能让它失效。
  - 机器人停止超过 10 分钟，或者桥接不再保留这个会话时，会话随之结束。
- **Claude 桌面版收尾卡片里的提问**：Claude 桌面版用收尾卡片问你问题时，会话显示“等你回答”和那个问题，不再显示“已完成”。

### 变化

- **一轮结束、后台还有任务时，显示“已完成”**：以前显示成绿色的“后台运行中”，看起来像还在干活。现在按“已完成”显示，未读、有浮窗，第二行写明后台任务；后台任务把 Claude 唤醒后，再显示“工作中”。
- **不再超时**：“等你回答”不会在 60 分钟后变成“闲置”，会一直等你回复，过夜也不会丢。

### 修复

- **跳到别的应用，不会再把 Claude 桌面版的对话算成已读**：Claude 桌面版的窗口在别的应用后面露出来时，会把窗口里显示的对话记成“看过”，比如点 Codex 浮窗、Codex 盖到浏览器上面的时候。现在只有 Claude 桌面版确实在前台时，这个记录才算已读。
