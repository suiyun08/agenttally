## AgentTally 1.0.6 (代理指示)

### Changed

- **The floating indicator shows its notices; the menu bar dots open the panel.** Each works the
  same way: rest the pointer on it to show, move away to put away, or click to keep them out and
  click again to put them away. Clicking the floating indicator no longer opens the panel.
  **Settings › General › Open on Hover** covers both.
- **Floating notices line up with the floating indicator** and move with it, under the capsule, or
  above it when the capsule sits in the lower half of the screen. They can no longer be dragged
  away on their own; a position they were dragged to before is forgotten.
- **The panel opens lined up under the menu bar dots** (or the notch pill) every time. You can
  still drag it while it is open; the next time it opens, it is back under the dots.
- **Floating notices stay folded until there is something to see.** They show for 5 seconds when a
  session finishes or stops on an error, and stay out for as long as a session waits for your
  permission or answer, folding away once you have answered. A click on the floating indicator
  puts waiting notices away until another session starts waiting.
- **"N more" shows every notice**, scrolling when they run past the screen, and "Show less" goes
  back. It used to open the panel.

### Fixed

- While the pointer rests on the notices, a new notice waits behind the "N more" count instead of
  shifting the notices under the pointer.
- After hours of use, the notices could stay behind on one desktop, so notices you kept out were
  missing when you switched to a full-screen app. They now follow you to every desktop.

## AgentTally 1.0.6（代理指示）

### 变化

- **悬浮胶囊只管浮窗，菜单栏指示灯只管面板**：两者用法相同，鼠标停上去展开、移开收起；点一下常驻，再点一下收起。
  点悬浮胶囊不再打开面板。「设置 › 通用 › 悬停展开」同时控制两者。
- **浮窗始终和悬浮胶囊对齐**，跟着胶囊移动：胶囊在屏幕上半部时排在它下方，在下半部时排在它上方。浮窗不能再单独拖走，
  以前拖到别处记住的位置会被清掉。
- **面板每次打开都对齐到菜单栏指示灯下方**（或刘海旁的指示灯下方）。打开后仍然可以拖动；下次打开时回到指示灯下方。
- **浮窗平时收起，有事才展开**：会话完成或出错停下时展开 5 秒；有会话在等你授权或等你回答时一直展开，处理完自动收起。
  点一下悬浮胶囊可以先收起这些等待中的浮窗，直到又有新的会话开始等你。
- **「还有 N 条」改为展开全部浮窗**，超出屏幕时可以滚动，点「收起」恢复。以前它会打开面板。

### 修复

- 鼠标停在浮窗上时，新出现的浮窗会排到「还有 N 条」里，不再把鼠标下面的浮窗挤开。
- 用了几个小时后，浮窗可能只留在一个桌面上，切到全屏应用时常驻的浮窗不见了。现在浮窗会跟着你出现在每个桌面上。
