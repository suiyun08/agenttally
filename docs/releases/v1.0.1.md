## AgentTally 1.0.1 (代理指示)

### Changed

- **A hover peek now always closes when the pointer leaves**, even after you click a session or
  a button in it. Only a panel opened by clicking the menu bar dots or the toggle shortcut stays
  open. Arrow keys and Escape still work in a peek once you click into it.

## AgentTally 1.0.1（代理指示）

### 变化

- **悬停展开的面板，鼠标移出后一定会自动收起**，在面板里点过会话或按钮也一样。只有点击菜单栏指示灯或按快捷键
  打开的面板才会常驻。在预览面板里点过之后，方向键和 Esc 照常可用。
