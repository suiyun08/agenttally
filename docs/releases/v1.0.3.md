## AgentTally 1.0.3 (代理指示)

### New

- **Floating indicator.** Settings › General › Floating Indicator shows the menu bar's dots on a
  small glass capsule you can drag anywhere. It stays on every desktop and over full-screen apps,
  remembers where you put it, and opens the panel the moment the pointer rests on it; a click keeps
  the panel open.

### Changed

- **Larger menu bar dots**, sized to sit level with other apps' icons.
- **A new glass for the panel**: Notification Center's Liquid Glass, kept clear while AgentTally
  is in the background, with white text in three levels that stays readable over bright and dark
  windows alike. Theme colors now set the status and accent colors.
- **No more light or dark switch** in Settings › Appearance: the glass decides.
- **The panel shows on every desktop**, so a pinned panel stays in view as you switch desktops.
- **Hover peeks fade in** with a short slide out of the dots they open from.

### Fixed

- The panel's glass no longer changes when the panel gains or loses focus.

## AgentTally 1.0.3（代理指示）

### 新增

- **悬浮指示灯**：「设置 › 通用 › 悬浮指示灯」会把菜单栏的指示灯放到一个可以拖到任意位置的玻璃小胶囊里。
  所有桌面和全屏应用上都能看到，会记住你放的位置；鼠标移上去面板立即展开，点一下面板就常驻。

### 变化

- **菜单栏指示灯放大**，和其他应用的图标大小相当。
- **面板换了新的玻璃**：采用“通知中心”样式的液态玻璃，代理指示在后台时也保持通透；文字统一为白色三级，
  在亮色和暗色窗口上方都看得清。主题配色改为决定状态色和强调色。
- **去掉了「设置 › 外观」里的浅色/深色开关**，由玻璃自己决定。
- **面板在所有桌面上显示**，固定打开后切换桌面也一直在。
- **悬停展开时带一个短的淡入动画**，从指示灯方向滑出。

### 修复

- 点击面板或切到其他应用时，面板玻璃的透明度不再跟着变化。
